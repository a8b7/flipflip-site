隐私政策 Privacy Policy

FlipFlip 翻翻 · 最后更新 Last updated:2026 年 10 月 9 日

一句话版本:FlipFlip 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。所有照片处理都在你的设备本地完成。 TL;DR: FlipFlip does not collect, upload, or share any of your data. All photo processing happens locally on your device.

1. 我们收集哪些信息What We Collect

什么都不收集。FlipFlip:

  • 没有账号系统,不要求注册或登录; No account system — no sign-up or sign-in required;
  • 没有自己的服务器,不联网传输照片; No servers of its own — your photos are never transmitted over the network;
  • 不嵌入任何统计、广告或第三方分析 SDK; No analytics, ads, or third-party tracking SDKs embedded;
  • 不获取你的位置、通讯录、麦克风或相机。 No access to your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

We collect nothing. FlipFlip has no accounts, no servers, no tracking SDKs, and never accesses your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

2. 照片权限的使用Photo Permission

App 请求「照片」访问权限,仅用于以下本地功能:

  • 随机读取你相册中的照片与视频,以卡片形式展示; Reading photos and videos from your library at random and showing them as cards;
  • 根据你的操作收藏照片,或把照片加入待删清单并执行删除; Favoriting photos, or adding them to a delete list and deleting them at your instruction;
  • 你标记的待删清单、偏好设置,只保存在设备本地(App 沙盒内),不产生任何网络请求。 Your delete list and preferences are stored only on your device (inside the app sandbox) — no network requests are ever made.

你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中收回或缩小授权范围。

FlipFlip requests photo access only for the local features above. You can change or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

3. 删除照片的说明About Deletion

删除操作由你逐一确认后执行,被删除的照片会进入 iOS 系统相册的「最近删除」,30 天内可随时恢复。FlipFlip 不会绕过系统确认机制,也不会在未经你确认的情况下修改你的相册。

Photos are deleted only after you confirm each action. Deleted photos move to the iOS “Recently Deleted” album, where they can be restored for 30 days. FlipFlip never bypasses the system confirmation step and never modifies your library without your consent.

4. 儿童Children

FlipFlip 不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息,也不包含任何用户生成内容的公开分享功能。

FlipFlip does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 and contains no public user-generated content sharing.

5. 政策变更Changes to This Policy

若未来版本引入任何数据处理变化,我们会在发布更新的同时修订本页面并在 App 内显著提示。

If any data-handling practice ever changes in a future version, we will revise this page and clearly notify you within the app when the update ships.

6. 联系我们Contact Us

对本政策或隐私实践有任何疑问,请联系:support@a8b7.com

If you have any questions about this policy or our privacy practices, contact us at support@a8b7.com.
