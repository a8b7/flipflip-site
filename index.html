快速上手Quick Start

  • 上滑 —— 把照片放入待删除清单 Swipe up — add the photo to the delete list
  • 下滑 —— 收藏 / 取消收藏 Swipe down — favorite / unfavorite
  • 左滑 / 右滑 —— 下一张 / 上一张 Swipe left / right — next / previous photo
  • 点按 —— 查看大图 Tap — view full screen
  • 右上角 ⚙ —— 设置翻片范围与规则 ⚙ (top-right) — set deck range & rules

Deletions go to a pending list first and only take effect after you confirm them in bulk. Deleted photos remain in the system “Recently Deleted” album for 30 days.

常见问题FAQ

  • 我的照片会上传吗?
    不会。FlipFlip 完全在本地运行,没有服务器、没有账号、没有统计 SDK。 Will my photos be uploaded?
    No. FlipFlip runs entirely on your device — no servers, no accounts, no analytics SDKs.
  • 误标了怎么办?
    在待删清单里左滑即可移出;已删除的照片在系统「最近删除」保留 30 天。 Marked something by mistake?
    Swipe left in the pending list to remove it. Deleted photos stay in “Recently Deleted” for 30 days.
  • 只能看到部分照片?
    系统授权为「已选照片」时,可在顶部横幅点「选择更多照片」扩充范围。 Only seeing some photos?
    If access is set to “Selected Photos”, tap “Select More Photos” in the banner at the top to expand it.
